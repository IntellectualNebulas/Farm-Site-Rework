Walks an d Play Parks

Explore, Play & Make Memories

At Murray Farms, there's plenty to discover beyond the farmyard. Whether you're looking for a peaceful countryside stroll, a place for the little ones to burn off some energy, or simply a chance to enjoy the great outdoors, our walks and play parks have something for everyone

Countryside Walks

Take a wander through the beautiful surroundings of Murray Farms and enjoy the sights, sounds and fresh air of the countryside. Our walking routes wind through open fields, leafy paths and scenic spots, offering a relaxing escape for visitors of all ages. Keep your eyes peeled as you explore — you never know what farm animals or wildlife you might spot along the way! Our walks are perfect for a leisurely family stroll, a refreshing morning walk or simply taking some time to slow down and enjoy nature.

Farm Play Park

Let the little ones run, climb, swing and slide at our fun-filled farm play park! Designed with adventurous young visitors in mind, the play area is a great place to let imaginations run wild. From climbing and balancing to racing down the slides, there's plenty to keep children entertained while parents can relax and enjoy the surrounding countryside

A Day Out for Everyone

Whether you're exploring the walking trails, enjoying a picnic in the fresh air or watching the children play, Murray Farms is all about making the most of time outdoors. Come along, explore the farm, enjoy the fresh air and make some memories together at Murray Farms.
