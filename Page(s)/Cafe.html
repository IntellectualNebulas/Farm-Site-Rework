Our Cafe

Tucked away at the heart of Murray Farm, our café is a place to slow down, enjoy good company, and make the most of the countryside around you.

We believe the best meals start with simple, honest ingredients and a little care. From freshly prepared breakfasts and homemade lunches to delicious cakes and a great cup of coffee, there's something for everyone to enjoy.

Whether you're joining us after a walk around the farm, meeting friends for a catch-up, or simply looking for somewhere peaceful to enjoy a coffee and a bite to eat, Murray Farm Café is the perfect place to pause and unwind.
